## Creative patterns
1. **Art-directed variety, stable hierarchy:** multiple palettes and art directions, but repeated large headline + small supporting elements.
2. **Mixed-media staging:** vector shapes, photos, device mockups, and editorial cards function as interchangeable layers.
3. **Camera-driven transitions:** compositional scaling and shifts are useful references for zoom/pan/parallax, but actual camera method cannot be identified from the rendered pixels alone.
4. **Designed rhythm:** sequence alternates busier shots with simpler presentations; test whether a calmer frame before dense frames helps comprehension.
5. **Focus before decoration:** the main takeaway for AD Factory is how strong typographic hierarchy organizes otherwise complex layouts.

## Avoid copying
Do not reuse proprietary brand logos, exact layouts, photography, characters, or slogans. Extract general motion/graphic principles only.

## Proposed reusable techniques
- `editorial-type-focus`: primary headline lands before supporting chips/shapes.
- `poster-stack-depth`: layered poster/cards with subtle offset, scale and parallax.
- `grid-to-hero`: tiled panels reorganize into one full-size focal item.
- `collage-mask-reveal`: photo and typography appear through opposing masks.
- `contrast-cut-reset`: alternate dense frames with sparse graphic frames.

Each technique is a **proposal**, not yet coded, tested, or approved for production.
